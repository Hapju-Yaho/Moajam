wrappers into JSON. The encoded MP3 recordings are unchanged. Playback applies
note timing, pitch, articulation and user volume. Decoded instrument samples
receive a stereo-linked fixed gain targeting 0.85 peak amplitude (up to 16x).
See manifest.json for the unchanged packaged MP3 data hashes.
